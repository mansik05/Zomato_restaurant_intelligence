# Exploratory Hypotheses – Zomato Restaurant Intelligence

## Hypothesis 1: Cost and Customer Feedback
Restaurants with higher estimated dining costs may be associated with higher average ratings and positive sentiment shares.

## Hypothesis 2: Sentiment and Numeric Rating
Restaurants with higher positive sentiment shares may also have higher numeric ratings.

## Hypothesis 3: Cuisine Variety
Restaurants offering a greater variety of cuisines may exhibit differences in average cost and customer feedback.

## Hypothesis 4: Negative Sentiment
Restaurants with higher negative sentiment shares may tend to have lower average numeric ratings.

## Notes
These hypotheses are exploratory and are based on observed associations in the dataset. They do not establish causation.
